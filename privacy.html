In short
Huereka controls your Philips Hue Bridge directly on your home network. There is no account, no advertising, no usage analytics and no crash reporting to the developer. The developer receives no data from you.

What the app processes and where it stays

  • Bridge key. When you pair, the bridge issues an application key. It is stored encrypted in the credential locker of your Windows account and never leaves this PC.
  • State of your home. Names of rooms, zones, lights, scenes and automations and their current state are read from the bridge in order to display them. They are kept in memory only while the app is running.
  • Settings. Tile order, hidden sections, background and custom colours are saved in the app's local folder on this PC. If you choose your own background picture, the app stores a downscaled copy there; the original stays untouched.
  • Error log. If an error occurs, the app writes a text file to the same local folder. It contains technical details (time, error message, the bridge's IP address on your home network) and is never transmitted.

Connections

  • To the bridge. All commands and the event stream run encrypted (HTTPS) between this PC and the bridge on your local network.
  • Local discovery. To find the bridge, the app uses mDNS; this stays on your local network.
  • Signify discovery service. During setup, or when the bridge has changed its address, the app also queries https://discovery.meethue.com, at most once every 15 minutes. Signify sees the public IP address of your internet connection and returns the local address and ID of your bridge. Signify's privacy notice applies to this processing: https://www.philips-hue.com/privacy

The app makes no other connections to the internet. Updates are delivered through the Microsoft Store, subject to Microsoft's privacy statement.

Legal basis
Processing serves only to provide the lighting control you ask for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). As the developer receives no personal data, no processing takes place on the developer's side.

Deletion
"Remove pairing" in the settings deletes the key. Uninstalling the app makes Windows remove the local app folder with settings and log. You can remove the app's entry from the bridge in the Hue app or at account.meethue.com.

Your rights
Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Contact the developer at support@animatex.de.

Children
The app is not directed at children and collects no data about them.

Changes
If what the app processes changes, this policy is updated before the release; the date above shows the current version.

Requires a Philips Hue Bridge (v2 or Bridge Pro) on the same network.
